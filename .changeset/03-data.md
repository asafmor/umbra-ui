**Data.** `Tag`, `Badge`, `ValuePill`, `Avatar`, `AvatarGroup`, `EntityIcon`, `KeyValue`, `KeyValueList`, `StatValue`, `SummaryTile`, `TrendArrow`, `StatusIndicator`, `GroupHeader`, `List`, `ListItem`, `Banner`, `Legend`, `EmptyState`, `Link`, `SequenceDots` (a short row of dots or bars for recent outcomes, with a shape per tone), `MediaCard` (an article or item card: `hero`, `compact` and `pair`, the whole card a link or a button with its actions apart) and `Comparison` (two sides compared on metrics, as an accessible table with bars from the centre, a summary and a one-line strip). Data components share `loading`, `empty`, `error` and `onRetry`, and values can flash when they change (`highlightChanges`). A row with a repeated id is drawn, with a warning in development.
