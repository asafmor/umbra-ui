**Meters.** `ProgressBar`, `LevelMeter`, `PieIndicator` (a small disc for cells and rows), `DivergingMeter` (a bar to one side of an origin or the other) and `MeterList` (any of the four in aligned columns of labels, bars and values). A value that is not a finite number is "no value", which never looks like zero.
