Added `SequenceDots` (a short row of dots or bars for recent outcomes, with a shape per tone), `MediaCard` (an article or item card: `hero`, `compact` and `pair`, the whole card a link or button with its actions separate) and `Comparison` (two sides compared on metrics, as an accessible table with bars from the centre, a summary header and a one-line strip).

Added `StarRating`, `SplitButton`, `Slider`, `Stepper` and `ColorScaleEditor`. `ColorScaleEditor` edits the same `thresholds` list that `ValuePill`, `ProgressBar`, `LevelMeter`, `BarChart`, `DonutChart` and `Slider` take. New built-in icon `star`.

Added `Spotlight` (a coach mark or a multi-step tour that dims everything but one element) and `ShortcutsSheet` (the keyboard help overlay, opened by `?`, listing the provider's shortcuts in groups with a search). `Shortcut` takes an optional `group`. New token `--umbra-layer-spotlight`, between the modal and the command palette.
