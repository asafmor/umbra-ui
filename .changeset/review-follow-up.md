`Button` and `IconButton` take `loadingLabel`, announced while loading. Filled buttons darken on hover so their text keeps its contrast. Resting nav items and the focus ring on the `TopBar` are computed from the brand colour. Selected tabs, the current row of a nav dropdown and toggled buttons carry a cue that is not colour. The scroll thumb is more visible. A custom card `bandColor` gets a readable text colour.
