`DataTable`, `Select`, `SearchField`, `Modal`, `ConfirmDialog`, `CommandPalette`, `PrimaryAction`, `HistoryButtons` and `ShortcutBar`. One icon map on the provider covers every built-in icon. Tabs, filters and headers move what does not fit into a "more" menu. A card collapsed with its caret animates its size. Renamed for consistency: `List` `selectedKeys` to `selectedIds`, `level` to `headingLevel` on `SectionTitle` and `GroupHeader`, and `ValuePill` `format` / `emptyText` to `formatValue` / `emptyLabel`.
